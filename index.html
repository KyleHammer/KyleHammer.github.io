Project Quiver

Delve through the caves of Quiver and see how far you can survive. Project Quiver is a simple Roguelike platformer that features procedurally generated levels with a variety of different items to assist you in your adventure.

Project Quiver was developed as part of my Master's Thesis, Procedural Content Generation for Emergent Gameplay. Through a series of playtests on Project Quiver, I evaluated the effectiveness of a PCG environment on emergent gameplay. To do this, I developed a framework that rates emergent factors and observed playtester actions for any emergent interactions.

That One Samurai Game

A hack N slash game where you play as a lone samurai against the world. In this game, you utilise well-timed strikes, dodges, and perfect parries to get past your enemies' defences.

That One Samurai Game was my capstone project for my Bachelor of Games Development degree at the University of Technology Sydney. It was developed by a team of six people, with my role in the project being a generalist programmer and project manager. My most notable contributions include developing the enemy behaviour system and particle systems.

Say Bananas

A brightly-themed platformer that uses learning principles to engage children in high-quality, home-practice speech therapy. Say Bananas is the first game developed by the Say66 team and is available on Android Tablets and iPads.

Work on Say Bananas has improved my proficiency in the Unity Engine and allowed me to experience what it is like to develop games to client specifications. The project has taught me new skills such as connecting a game to external services like Firebase and Azure, and has given me experience in deploying apps to multiple storefronts and devices.

Game Jams and Collaborative Projects

Outside of work, I like to develop new projects and participate in game jams, which has been a great opportunity to work with other developers and hone my own skills. I most commonly work as a project manager and programmer on these projects, with a bit of design and Q/A testing in the mix as well. You can check out a list of my games on my itch page. Some notable projects include the following:

  • Coffee Jitters - Play as a cafe customer with high anxiety who attempts to get their coffee order without stuttering or making mistakes. A game developed for the 2023 GMTK game jam
  • Crystal Mech - Traverse the deepest depths of the randomly generated mine in this top-down, arcade-like experience, using the randomly generated weapons to ensure your survival.
  • Soul Searcher - A turn-based strategy game with procedurally generated dungeons where you delve through a passing man's memories to find his soul.

Experience @ UTS

In 2020 I graduated from the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) with a Bachelor of Science in Games Development. My interest in game development continued and by 2023 I had completed a Master's by Research specialising in Procedural Content Generation and Emergent Gameplay.

Throughout my bachelor's, I used Agile game development practices and worked with other talented individuals to create new and exciting games. I was later fortunate enough to be an integral part of the Playmakers Game Development Society at UTS, where I would go on to hold game jams for fellow game dev students and even host my own workshops.

Running game development workshops later evolved into work at UTS, as I started to tutor a mix of game development subjects ranging from Introduction to Computer Game Development to Game Design Studio.
